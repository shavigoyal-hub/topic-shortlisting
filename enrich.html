Cannibalization

Each page → its top GSC query → top-10 SERP. Two pages cannibalize when their top-10 URLs overlap ≥ threshold (same intent). Winner = whoever ranks live; impressions break ties.

Two checks: Duplicate Intent (all pages sharing ≥3 queries) and Active Cannibalization (feed↔main splits — ≥40% balanced, both ranking ≤20, confirmed on live Google). Weak = <10 impressions. Both apply the unique-value guard: never redirect a page that ranks for queries no other page owns.
GSC/All modes fetch one live SERP per unique top query — can be a few hundred Serper calls on large sites (cached after the first run).
